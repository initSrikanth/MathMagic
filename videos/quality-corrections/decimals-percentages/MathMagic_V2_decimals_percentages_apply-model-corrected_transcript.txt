Compare the share of each total by renaming it on a common scale.

Percentages use a scale of one hundred; they do not tell us how many items there were originally.

Team A wins eight out of ten games.

Rename the share as eighty out of one hundred, which is eighty percent.

Team B wins seventy-five out of one hundred games, which is seventy-five percent.

Eighty percent is greater, so Team A has the greater relative success, although Team B has more wins in total.
