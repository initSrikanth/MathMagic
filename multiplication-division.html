Multiply Efficiently

Use multiplication facts, place value and properties of operations.

KEY IDEA

Break a large number into place-value parts, multiply each part, then combine the partial products.

Worked example

324 × 8
= (300 × 8) + (20 × 8) + (4 × 8)
= 2,400 + 160 + 32
= 2,592

Try it

Calculate 426 × 7.

Reason

Why does partitioning 324 into 300 + 20 + 4 preserve the value of 324 × 8?

Divide by a Single-digit Number

KEY IDEA

Division can mean sharing equally or finding how many groups fit. Use multiplication facts, place value and the inverse relationship.

Worked example

1,536 ÷ 6
1,500 ÷ 6 = 250
36 ÷ 6 = 6
So 1,536 ÷ 6 = 256

Try it

Calculate 2,184 ÷ 7.

Check with the inverse

256 × 6 = 1,536, so 1,536 ÷ 6 = 256 is consistent.

PAGE 4

Interpret Remainders

The context decides what the remainder means.

Round up

436 passengers travel on buses holding 50 people. 436 ÷ 50 = 8 remainder 36, so 9 buses are needed.

Express the remainder as a fraction

23 pizzas shared equally among 4 groups gives 5 remainder 3, or 5 34 pizzas per group.

Express the remainder as a decimal

23 litres shared equally among 4 containers gives 5 remainder 3. Because 34 = 0.75, each container receives 5.75 L.

Try it

67 students form teams of 8. How many teams are needed so every student has a team?

Reason

Why is “8 remainder 3” sometimes not a complete answer to a real-world problem?

Unknowns, Estimation & Modelling

INVERSE RELATIONSHIP

If 48 × 25 = 1,200, then 1,200 ÷ 25 = 48. Use equivalent number sentences and properties to find unknown values.

Missing value

□ × 24 = 1,680
1,680 ÷ 24 = 70

Reasonableness

398 × 21 is close to 400 × 20 = 8,000, so an exact answer near 8,000 is reasonable.
