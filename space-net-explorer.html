Cube Net Explorer

Learning goal: Predict whether six connected squares will fold into a cube.

Success criteria: I can select six edge-connected squares; predict which faces will meet; explain why some arrangements overlap when folded.

Select or deselect squares to design a net. Use Tab and Space/Enter for keyboard access. Then press Test folding. Every valid cube net must have exactly six squares, be edge-connected, and fold to six different cube faces.

Try the example net, then design your own.

How the folding test works

Imagine one square as the front face of a cube. When folding across a shared edge, the next square turns 90 degrees. If two squares would land on the same cube face, the net overlaps. A correct net reaches all six different face directions.
