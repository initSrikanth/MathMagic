Space Lab: move the shape

Learning goal: Explore translations, reflections and rotations using a shape you can move.

Success criteria: I can slide a shape without turning it; I can reflect it across a mirror line; I can rotate it around a point and describe what changes.

Drag the orange shape, use the arrow buttons, or focus the shape and press the arrow keys. Try a transformation and explain which properties stay the same.

Explore: the triangle has three sides and three vertices.

Think: Does sliding, turning or reflecting change the triangle's side lengths? How is a reflection different from a rotation?
