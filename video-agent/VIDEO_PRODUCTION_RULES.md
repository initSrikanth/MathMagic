# Video production rules

The single authoritative policy is [video-production-contract.json](video-production-contract.json), enforced by [contract.py](../tools/video-quality/contract.py), guarded generation/integration entry points and CI.

It applies to Year 5, Year 6 and Year 7 teaching videos. Former presenter/HeyGen defaults here are superseded: graphics-only, no paid services, no in-video branding, and a natural, warm female Indian-English teaching voice with modest enthusiasm.

Keep new-topic production suspended. The founder authorises objectively verified branding-only and independent mathematical/visual corrections to existing videos without waiting for unrelated accent review. Use the contract’s narrowly scoped correction gate, preserving original voices/files and explicitly UNVERIFIED accent/full-quality status. Accent-dependent replacements and full-quality approval still require genuine listening/viewing evidence for the exact final bytes. CI, ASR, OCR and voice identifiers cannot certify accent, expressiveness or teaching quality.

Preserve satisfactory Measurement narration and original media for rollback. Review-only packet-preserving branding edits do not approve an unsuitable voice, authorise synthesis or waive release gates. Never time-stretch speech, insert silence to accommodate graphics, substitute an unverified accent or describe a scoped correction as full perceptual approval. Preserve quiz, authentication and progress functionality.
