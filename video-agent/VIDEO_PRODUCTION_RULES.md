# MathMagic video production rules

1. Mathematics is the focus. Keep the approved teacher full-body, unframed, on the left.
2. Reuse approved HeyGen footage and voice; do not regenerate the presenter or spend credits unnecessarily.
3. Draw mathematical equations, fraction bars and number lines with programmatic graphics, not HeyGen.
4. Every change in spoken mathematical explanation must have a corresponding on-screen scene; a single equation must never remain for an entire video.
5. Use the correct lesson scene definitions: Comparing Fractions must never use Equivalent Fractions slides.
6. All fraction comparisons and equivalences must be checked mathematically, with equal-size wholes and legible horizontal fraction bars.
7. Synchronise scenes with narration; inspect start, scene boundaries and ending.
8. Avoid unnecessary spoken introductions such as "Welcome to MathMagic". Trim only when existing audio permits without damaging the explanation.
9. Avoid framed avatars, clipped feet, unreadable equations or distracting animations.
10. Future HeyGen prompts should request modestly more facial expression and subtle natural hand/body movement.
11. Export MP4 using H.264 yuv420p and AAC, suitable for mobile Safari.
12. Keep existing videos live until replacements are rendered, checked, uploaded and verified.
13. Confirm the actual MP4 exists on the target GitHub branch and is referenced by the Fractions player before reporting publication.
14. Run mathematical checks, representative-frame review, duration/audio checks and live-browser playback.
15. Do not change authentication, progress tracking, or unrelated lessons.
16. Retain reversible source footage and scene-generation code.
17. Publish one lesson at a time; provide a working commit and the live video URL.
18. Never report success based solely on a workflow or HTML commit.

19. Never insert artificial silence, frozen teacher frames, sentence holds, audio stretching or unnatural narration interruptions. Synchronise persistent graphics to the continuous original audio; transition graphics only.
20. For explicitly authorised graphics-only lessons, omit the avatar. Verify the exact requested provider voice and settings; never substitute a voice or route synthesis through HeyGen when HeyGen credits are prohibited. Keep silent drafts clearly labelled and unreviewed videos off the live lesson.
