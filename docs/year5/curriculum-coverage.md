## Method and limits

- **COMPLETE**: Every required curriculum skill has identifiable source teaching, activity/investigation and aligned assessment evidence. This is resource coverage, not pupil mastery, full media approval or live-service certification.
- **PARTIAL**: Relevant implemented resources exist, but one or more required skills, assessed reasoning forms or assessment-validity requirements have a concrete gap.
- **MISSING**: No implemented resources establish this descriptor.
- **UNVERIFIED**: Potential coverage exists but cannot be established from inspected evidence. Never counted complete.

Coverage is independent of student progress, media approval and deployment. A COMPLETE descriptor does not certify a released strand. Practical building, measurement and open investigation performance require pupil/teacher observation. Source/transcript inspection and public byte verification do not constitute new full listening/viewing.
