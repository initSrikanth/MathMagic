# Year 5 strand completion plan

The [24-descriptor matrix](curriculum-coverage.md) and [machine-readable evidence](curriculum-coverage.json) replace topic-title counting for current planning. The 4 November coverage files remain historical. This task builds navigation and an audit; it does not develop a new topic, produce videos, migrate Firebase or deploy the new dashboard.

## Permanent development rules

1. Before content development, map every descriptor and achievement-standard aspect against official final ACARA/QCAA v9 sources. Record component skills, actual lesson/anchor, tool, assessment family/validation, video/transcript, gaps and corrective action. Recheck sources when scope changes; consultation-draft codes and topic names are not evidence.
2. Complete **Number → Algebra → Measurement → Space → Statistics → Probability**. Finish and verify one strand before starting new content in the next. Existing later-strand resources remain available and are preserved. Routine fixes to an existing topic do not authorise another topic build.
3. COMPLETE means evidenced curriculum resource coverage. Never infer it from five lessons, a green quiz or a video filename. Maintain development, media approval, QA, live-service verification and deployment independently. A strand cannot be marked released until every descriptor is COMPLETE and every mandatory release gate has evidence.
4. Newly developed topics normally have five coherent constructivist lessons where educationally appropriate: hook, goal, three success criteria, prior knowledge, concrete/pictorial/abstract teaching, worked examples and prediction, investigation, misconception correction and reflection. Retain justified existing structures (Space has ten); share resources and do not manufacture duplicate lessons or quizzes to reach a count.
5. Provide accessible interactive learning tools and a mathematically validated 30-question assessment across six genuine difficulty bands. Map each family to teaching, check duplicates/equivalent answers and worked feedback. Use open-task rubrics/teacher observation for creation, modelling, practical measurement/building and investigation performance; a numeric quiz score cannot substitute for those skills.
6. Instructional videos must meet every current Golden Rule and the unchanged canonical [production contract](../../video-agent/video-production-contract.json). Follow exact voice, mathematical, synchronisation, caption/transcript, accessibility, technical and exact-file human approval requirements. The unrelated-topic production hold remains active. This audit authorises no synthesis or rendering and grants no new media approval. Preserve approved Statistics/Space files and checksums.
7. Reuse the static HTML/CSS/JS architecture, existing topic IDs, URLs, authentication/approval gates and progress paths. Do not reset, rename or migrate records merely to change navigation. Shared topics keep their original assessment history; Number and Algebra links to Multiplication & Division use the same record.
8. Run automatic matrix/link/claim checks, generator validation appropriate to changed families, browser accessibility/layout checks at 390/768/desktop and existing-topic regressions. Keep Chromium/WebKit and isolated Firebase/emulator evidence distinct from physical iOS and live Firebase. Preserve all security checks and the protected-only Multiplication & Division service.
9. Before release, review exact-head CI, approvals and deployment evidence; verify actual published resources, authorised authentication/progress and old topics. No Year 6 development until **all six Year 5 strands** are complete, verified and deployed. Other Years 1–10 are navigation placeholders only and remain disabled.
10. Update matrix, resource inventory and this backlog with each coherent committed checkpoint. Run `python3 tools/curriculum/build_pages.py` after metadata changes and `npm run test:curriculum`. Do not treat generated pages or this plan as proof content was completed.

## Reproducible assessment-quality audit

`node tools/curriculum/inspect_assessments.cjs` inspects 1,000 deterministic 30-position attempts for each of Whole Numbers, Decimals & Percentages, Addition & Subtraction and protected Multiplication & Division: **120,000 positions**. It found respectively **1,340 / 1,013 / 1 / 10 exact duplicate positions**, and **78 equal-plan comparisons** with unsuitable “larger plan” wording. [Exact seeds/examples](assessment-audit.json) are retained. This read-only probe is separate from existing passing regression tests, which do not check all these properties. It is not a proof of all mathematical answers or semantic duplicates. N01 and N04 are PARTIAL because their assessment reliability is not complete. These baseline defects are assigned to the next Number task; no assessment is rewritten here.
