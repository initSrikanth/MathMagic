# Addition & Subtraction — Year 5 build

## Curriculum boundary
This module consolidates efficient whole-number addition and subtraction strategies and applies them through estimation, reasonableness, missing-value/inverse reasoning and practical/financial modelling. Decimal addition/subtraction is excluded because it is explicit Year 6 content. Fraction addition/subtraction remains in the Fractions module.

## Progression
30 questions in six five-question bands: Foundation, Developing, Developing+, Proficient, Application and Challenge.

## Protected content
Callable start/submit endpoints require Firebase Auth, server approval and App Check. Sessions are UID/topic bound; answers/solutions are stripped from question payloads and marking is server-side.

## Release status
Repository/CI success does not mean the protected backend is deployed. Production cutover requires Firebase deployment, App Check enforcement and authorised/unauthorised real-session verification.
