# Multiplication & Division — Year 5

## Topic-exclusive boundary
Large whole numbers multiplied by one- and two-digit numbers; whole numbers divided by single-digit numbers; multiplication facts, place value and operation properties; estimation/reasonableness; inverse relationships and unknowns; practical modelling; interpret contextual remainders, including rounding decisions and mixed-fraction/decimal interpretations where appropriate. Decimal multiplication/division by natural numbers is excluded as Year 6 content.

## Assessment progression
30 questions: Foundation, Developing, Developing+, Proficient, Application, Challenge — five questions per band.

## Protection
Firebase callable start/submit endpoints require Auth, approval and App Check. Sessions are UID/topic bound; answers and solutions are removed from public question payloads; marking is server-side.

## Production caveat
Repository/CI validation does not establish production deployment. Firebase deployment, App Check enforcement and real authorised/unauthorised session checks remain separate.
