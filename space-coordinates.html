Coordinate Explorer

Mission: A rescue drone must find exact locations on a map. Move the orange point and describe its coordinates. The first number is the horizontal position (across); the second is the vertical position (up).

Learning goal: Construct and use a first-quadrant coordinate grid.

  1. I can identify the origin (0,0).
  2. I can read and plot ordered pairs (x,y).
  3. I can describe a route by changes in x and y.

Point: (2, 2)

Plot a target

Predict where (4,7) lies, then move the orange point.

Think: How are (3,8) and (8,3) different? Why must we say across before up?

Show a worked example

To plot (4,7), begin at (0,0), move four grid units right, then seven units up. The point lies at the intersection of those grid lines, not inside a square.
